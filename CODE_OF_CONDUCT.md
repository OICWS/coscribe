# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to issues, pull requests, comments, commit messages and any other
space where the project is discussed, and to people and AI sessions alike.

## Our standard

Expected:

- Be respectful and assume good faith; disagree with the work, not the person.
- Give and accept direct, specific feedback.
- Say plainly what you did and what you did not verify. Report failures as
  failures.
- Respect other people's time and in-flight work.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks,
publishing someone's private information, and deliberately misleading others
about what a change does or whether it was tested.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@OICWS](https://github.com/OICWS)) may edit or remove
comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these
rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their GitHub
profile; reports are handled privately and treated with care.
